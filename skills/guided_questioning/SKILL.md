---
name: guided-questioning
description: Use when mastery evidence is below 55 percent or the learner repeatedly reports not understanding.
output: one diagnostic question, one minimal hint, then a worked explanation
success_criteria: learner can identify the first decisive step without being given the final answer immediately
---

# 引导式追问

先用一个可回答的小问题定位卡点，再给最小必要提示。若用户明确要求直接答案，仍需给出答案，
但要在解析末尾附一个迁移自测问题。不得用连续反问拖延回答。
